About us

We are redefining transport across Uganda.

Secure, affordable rides across Kampala and beyond.

Our Guiding Compass

Every decision we make is rooted in making road transport safer, more accessible, and more reliable for every Ugandan.

Mission

To deliver safe, affordable, and dependable ride-hailing services that connect riders and drivers across Kampala and Uganda's growing cities.

Vision

To become Uganda's most trusted transportation platform — where every passenger feels secure and every driver earns with dignity.

Core Values

  • Innovation
  • Reliability
  • Customer Focus
  • Integrity
  • Excellence
How We Work

We combine smart app design with local operations and responsive support so every journey feels effortless.

Fast rider matching

We connect riders and vetted drivers quickly based on location, route, and availability.

Transparent trips

Every ride includes clear fare estimates, route visibility, and real-time updates.

Local support

Our Uganda-based support team is available around the clock to help riders and drivers.
